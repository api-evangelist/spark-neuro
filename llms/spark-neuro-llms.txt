## What the company does

SPARK Neuro measures brain activity with electroencephalography (EEG) and interprets it with
deep-learning models. The same measurement core is sold into two very different markets, plus a
third product built on healthcare claims data:

- **SPARK Medical** — objective, EEG-derived biomarkers for Alzheimer's disease, traumatic brain
  injury and concussion, Parkinson's disease, ALS, and mental-health conditions such as
  depression, anxiety and PTSD. The company states that FDA-cleared EEGs supply thousands of
  measurements per second, and ships a software platform that runs test protocols and generates
  reports at the push of a button, so non-specialists can administer them. Buyers are clinicians,
  researchers, and pharmaceutical drug-discovery and clinical-trial teams.
- **Spark MEDIA** — biometric measurement of emotional and attentional response to advertising
  and entertainment content, positioned as "tapping into the subconscious to get the truth."
- **Spark Predict** — a conversational analytics interface over the company's healthcare claims
  intelligence engine, marketed to healthcare payers and providers.

Leadership: Spencer Gerrol (Founder & CEO), Marinela Gombosev (President & COO), Harry Leider, MD
(Chief Strategy Officer), Richard Caselli, MD (Medical Director). The company reports raising more
than $35M. Headquarters: New York, NY.

## API surface

**There is none that is publicly documented.** As of 2026-08-28, an unauthenticated probe of every
SPARK Neuro host found no developer program of any kind:

- No developer portal, API reference, quickstart, or SDK page. `/developers`, `/api`, `/docs` and
  `/api-docs` all return 404 on sparkneuro.com.
- No machine-readable contract. `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`,
  `/v1/openapi.json` and `/redoc` return 404 on both sparkneuro.com and sparkpredict.ai.
- No GraphQL endpoint (`/graphql` → 404) and no MCP endpoint (`/mcp` → 404) on sparkpredict.ai.
- No `/.well-known/*` documents on any host — security.txt, openid-configuration,
  oauth-authorization-server, api-catalog, ai-plugin.json, agent-card.json and agent.json all 404.
- No first-party client libraries on npm (zero results for "sparkneuro") or PyPI (404).
- `api.sparkneuro.com` resolves in DNS but its origin returns **502 Bad Gateway** on every path,
  so nothing there is reachable or readable.
- The only machine-readable route index served anywhere is the WordPress core REST API
  (`/wp-json/`) that the CMS enables by default on the three marketing sites. That is website
  content plumbing, not a SPARK Neuro product API, and it is deliberately not cataloged as one.

The company's software reaches customers as a delivered clinical/analytics product under an
enterprise agreement, not as an API. An agent cannot integrate with SPARK Neuro programmatically
today; the entry point is a sales conversation.
